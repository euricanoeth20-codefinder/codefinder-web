How Store Owners Upload Bulk CSV Catalogs:

Instead of manually keying in hundreds of products, store managers prepare a standard spreadsheet with 6 columns:

Column A: Product Name (e.g. Artisan Sourdough Loaf 800g)
Column B: Barcode / PLU (e.g. 6001234567890 or 104)
Column C: Department (e.g. Bakery & Pastries)
Column D: Category (e.g. Artisan Breads)
Column E: Price ZAR (e.g. 38.50)
Column F: Image URL (Optional photo link)

In the Owner Console, click "📤 Upload Product CSV" to distribute items to staff terminals in under 2 seconds.
