How Store Owners & Managers Upload Product Catalogs

Save hours of manual data entry by uploading your entire store catalog in one spreadsheet.

1. Spreadsheet Column Structure (6 Columns):
  • Column A (Product Name): e.g. Artisan Sourdough Loaf 800g
  • Column B (Barcode / PLU): e.g. 6001234567890 or 104
  • Column C (Department): Bakery & Pastries, Butchery & Meats, etc.
  • Column D (Category): e.g. Breads, Beef, Cheeses
  • Column E (Price): Decimal price in Rands (e.g. 38.50 — no 'R' symbol)
  • Column F (Image URL): Picture link (optional)
2. Instant Department Isolation:

When imported, CodeFinder automatically distributes items to matching departments. Bakery cashiers never see butchery meats or hardware items!
